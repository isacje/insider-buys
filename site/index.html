This preview uses sample data for made-up companies. Real filings appear once the site is running on GitHub.

Who's buying their own company's stock

Executives and directors must report their trades to the SEC within two business days. We read every filing and highlight the purchases worth a closer look.

Several insiders buying the same stock

Three or more different insiders bought on the open market in the past 30 days. Each mark is one person.

    Recent trades

    Data comes from Form 4 filings on SEC EDGAR, updated every weekday morning. Open-market purchases and sales only; option exercises, grants and gifts are left out. Trades whose price per share looks mistyped in the filing are set aside, and the same trade filed twice is shown once.

    This is information, not investment advice. Insiders buy for many reasons, and a purchase doesn't mean the stock will rise.
